    Sync your recording

    1. Tap along. Press Start and the recording plays from the very beginning (use +5s to skip a long intro). Tap Space (or the pad) on the first beat of every bar, starting at bar 1. Tap as long as you like (the more bars, the tighter the sync, especially if the band speeds up), then press Stop.
    2. Fine-tune. Preview from bar 1. If the cursor runs ahead of the recording, shift later; if it lags, shift earlier.

    About Tab Trainer

    Tab Trainer shows Guitar Pro files as notation and tab and helps you practise them. Everything stays on your device: your files, recordings and practice history are never uploaded.
